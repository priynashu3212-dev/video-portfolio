Who is Priyanshu Rana?

Priyanshu Rana is a first-year college student and a self-taught digital marketer focused on using AI to get real work done — research, content, ad copy, campaign planning and reporting. He is based in Siwan, Kaithal, Haryana, and works with businesses across Kaithal and Haryana as well as clients anywhere online.

His work sits between two things he enjoys most: marketing that helps a business get found, and building the websites that make that possible. He builds the front end himself in HTML, CSS and JavaScript, and writes the copy that turns a visitor into an enquiry.

Every project is handled with a clear scope and a clear quote before any work starts. He is currently open to internships and freelance projects that fit around college hours.
